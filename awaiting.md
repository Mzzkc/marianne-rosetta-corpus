# Patterns Awaiting Primitives

Each row was rechecked against the source-verified Marianne primitives on 2026-09-03; iteration-6 rows added 2026-09-04 after three adversarial reviews (each blocked on a named absent primitive, per the corpus's no-fabrication rule). A nearby mechanism is not treated as the missing primitive: `cost_limits` is per-job, `fan_out` is parse-time static, and ordinary parallelism does not provide consumer-driven backpressure or multi-host authority. Rechecked 2026-10-20 during the primitives gap-report remediation (newly claimed: sheet_descriptions, hook_variables, score_logging, judgment, schedule_pinning, structured_output): no blocker is resolved — `schedule_pinning` is byte-digest admission, not offset scheduling; the `sheet_triggers` run action fires once on_fail and replaces retry, not per-attempt retry hooks; `checkpoints` are approval gates, not write-ahead mid-sheet state.

Rechecked 2026-10-21 against the 97-primitive inventory: OODA Pulse is re-proposable using active sheet triggers, bounded loops and flow expressions. Durable checkpoint persistence still supplies no mid-sheet transaction; CLI descendant supervision supplies no hierarchical restart tree. The remaining blockers retain their absent-capability boundary.

## Moved Out of Awaiting

- **OODA Pulse** was blocked on “A runtime feedback hook that can re-orient and alter the next execution cycle”. Active `sheet.triggers` run/goto actions, `sheet.loops` and `flow_expressions` now support a score-authored observe/decide cycle. Resolved 2026-10-21; the pattern is re-proposable next iteration, not promoted here.
- **Saga Compensation Chain** is now a working concert-level pattern: durable top-level `on_failure` hooks exist, including restart reconciliation and same-ID protection.
- **Stretto Entry** is superseded by **Canon of Phases**, which uses leased recurrence plus explicit multi-deployment rotation; the old overlapping-sheet name is retired rather than kept as a duplicate.
- **The Aboyeur** is superseded by **Firing the Pass**, which computes deadline-first dated inputs without claiming nonexistent offset-from-artifact scheduling.

## Re-Proposable Research Names

**Contact Point co-evolution** was removed from the iteration-5.1 monolith without a concrete blocking primitive or curated pattern. It is not an awaiting pattern today; a future iteration may re-propose it with a precise substrate contract.
